# Zer0pa

Zer0pa is a falsification-governed, AI-native research and development lab with a public proof surface.

We sit between an applied research lab, an IP foundry, a proof-infrastructure company, and a frontier lab. The work spans motion, music, markets, biosignals, neural events, ancient scripts, vessel design, in-silico science pipelines, and deterministic computation. Across that portfolio, the operating question is consistent: what can be bounded, encoded, tested, broken, replayed, and stated without hiding the limits?

We direct frontier AI systems as researchers, coders, scientific operators, and adversarial thinkers across public repositories. The aim is not one universal platform. The aim is a portfolio of bounded systems that connect scientific corridors: physics, code, falsification, and nature's design patterns turned into testable artifacts.

## Readiness Standard

Every public product or research front door should land in one of three states.

| Status | Meaning | Current count |
|---|---|---:|
| `RESEARCH-READY` | Public proof surface exists. Claims are bounded. Evidence, blockers, negative results, or next validation gates are named. Not a production or enterprise claim. | 25 |
| `DEVELOPER-READY` | A developer can install, inspect, run, or build against the stated surface under the repo's limits. Still not enterprise-ready unless explicitly stated. | 7 |
| `ENTERPRISE-READY` | Procurement, security, support, deployment, and operational evidence are ready for enterprise adoption. | 0 |

When in doubt, we classify a repo as `RESEARCH-READY`. No current public repo is classified as `ENTERPRISE-READY`.

## Operating Standard

Zer0pa repos are front doors, not pitch decks.

- Claims are bounded to the repo and its evidence surface.
- Negative results, blocked gates, stale packages, platform limits, and non-claims are first-class outputs.
- Product-readiness claims are kept separate from package-install mechanics.
- Public proof surfaces do not grant private code, datasets, weights, CAD, raw CFD, partner material, manufacturing authority, or internal runtime systems.
- The portfolio is built to be falsified: physics, code, receipts, and stated blockers before narrative.

## Licensing And Boundaries

Canonical legal endpoint: [ZPE-License-Commercial](https://github.com/Zer0pa/ZPE-License-Commercial).

## What We Do Not Claim

- We do not claim one universal algorithm across the portfolio.
- We do not claim production medical, regulatory, class, shipyard, manufacturing, or enterprise approval from research repos.
- We do not hide adverse results below the fold.
- We do not treat a package install as product readiness.
- We do not treat "live on GitHub" as a readiness category.
